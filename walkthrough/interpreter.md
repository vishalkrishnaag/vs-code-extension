## Pick the felidae interpreter

The extension runs your code with the `felidae` executable. It looks in the workspace, beside your file
and in its parent folders (`build/debug/x64/Debug`, `build/release`, `dist/bin`), then on `PATH`.

The **Felidae** item in the status bar shows which one a file will use, with its version.
Click it, or use the button below, to choose another or browse for one.
